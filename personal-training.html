Who it's for?

Personal training suits anyone who wants faster progress or more focus than a group class can give. Some people come to learn the basics without feeling watched. Others want to fix a habit in their footwork, build fitness for a goal, or prepare for their first sparring session. You don't need any experience, and you don't need your own gear to get started.

Every session starts with a quick chat about what you want to work on, followed by a warm-up. Then you work with your coach on pads, the heavy bag and technique drills, matched to your level. Your coach corrects your form as you go and ends the session with a cool-down, so you know what to practise next time.

Your first step is to tell us your goal and when you're free. We match you with a coach whose strengths fit what you're after, and we agree a plan together. As you improve, the plan changes with you, so you're always working at the right level.
